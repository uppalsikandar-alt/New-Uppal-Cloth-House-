Our Story

A Legacy Built on Quality Fabric

ہماری کہانی — معیار اور اعتماد کی

Since 1987, New Uppal Cloth House has been the trusted destination for premium unstitched men's fabrics. With 38 years of expertise, we bring you the finest washingwear, cotton, latha, boski and karandi from Pakistan's most respected brands.

  • 38+ years of experience in fine quality fabrics
  • Carefully selected brands known for superior craftsmanship
  • Wide variety of washingwear, cotton, latha, boski and karandi
  • Trusted by generations of families for premium unstitched cloth
  • Personalised service and expert fabric guidance
